Why AWS is this complicated

Four reasons, each counted from the AWS SDK, and one reason it is not a mistake.

03Where your things live

04Permission for every action

Nothing works until IAM allows it. Each service defines its own actions, and a policy has to name the right ones on the right resources. IAM's own API has 180 operations, more than 428 of the 436 APIs.

05Not a mistake

This complexity is not a mistake. AWS serves banks, streaming services and space agencies, and each service exists because some customer needed exactly that. The cost lands on everyone else: to put a few pages online, you meet the same menu.
