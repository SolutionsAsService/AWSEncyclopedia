CloudFront

Amazon CloudFront

SDK name
cloudfront
API operations
167
Size rank
#11 of 436

What it is

A content delivery network: copies of your files served from locations near your visitors, with HTTPS.

What it is for

Speed and HTTPS in front of S3, load balancers or any origin.

The catch

Distributions, origins, behaviours, cache policies, invalidations, and a certificate that must live in us-east-1.
