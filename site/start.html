The 13 basics

Out of 436, these are the services almost every AWS account touches. Each page says what it is, what it is for, the catch, and whether a website needs it.

  1. ComputeEC2Virtual servers you rent by the second. You choose the size, the operating system and the network, and you look after everything on it.811 operations · #1 of 436
  2. StorageS3Object storage: files (“objects”) in named “buckets”, reached by URL or API, stored across several data centres.116 operations · #25 of 436
  3. ComputeLambdaFunctions that run when something happens (a web request, a file upload, a message) without a server you manage. You pay per request and per millisecond.88 operations · #56 of 436
  4. DatabaseDynamoDBA key-value and document database that scales without servers. You design tables around the questions you will ask.62 operations · #105 of 436
  5. DatabaseRDSManaged relational databases: PostgreSQL, MySQL, MariaDB, Oracle, SQL Server, IBM Db2, and Amazon Aurora.168 operations · #10 of 436
  6. SecurityIAMIdentity and Access Management: users, roles and JSON policies that say who may do what to which resource.180 operations · #8 of 436
  7. MessagingSNSSimple Notification Service: publish one message, deliver it to many subscribers (queues, functions, email, SMS, HTTP).42 operations · #153 of 436
  8. MessagingSQSSimple Queue Service: a queue that holds messages until a worker takes them.23 operations · #264 of 436
  9. DeliveryCloudFrontA content delivery network: copies of your files served from locations near your visitors, with HTTPS.167 operations · #11 of 436
  10. ComputeElastic BeanstalkAWS Elastic Beanstalk: upload an app and it creates the servers, load balancer and scaling for you, on EC2.47 operations · #136 of 436
  11. DatabaseElastiCacheManaged in-memory caches (Valkey, Redis OSS, Memcached).77 operations · #70 of 436
  12. StorageEFSElastic File System: a shared network file system many servers can mount at once.31 operations · #202 of 436
  13. StorageEBSElastic Block Store: virtual disks attached to one EC2 server.6 operations · #382 of 436
